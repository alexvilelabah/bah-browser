Prices and availability only exist on booking sites — do not answer from memory or from search snippets.
1. No site named? Open a major booking site directly (navigate to its home page) rather than a search engine. Searching never needs an account: close sign-in popups (× or Escape) — never click "Sign in" / "Continue with Google". If a site still will not search, try another (Kayak, Expedia, Hotels.com, or one a web_search finds).
2. Fill the destination with site-search + autocomplete-pick, then the dates with date-picker (TWO clicks for a range), then guests. Dates: use the TODAY line; "next weekend" = the Coming weekend given there.
3. After searching, VERIFY the page shows the right place, dates and guests. Many booking sites keep them in the results URL (e.g. checkin=YYYY-MM-DD) — you can fix them there.
4. Sort by price (lowest first). Budget "under $X a night": if a price is for the whole stay, divide by the nights first.
5. Report 3–5 options: name, price per night (and total), rating, area, and the dates actually searched. Never reserve, book or enter personal or payment details.
